## 🎮 About the Game

the cabin game is a cooperative horror card game developed by Twisted Corridor. It entered Early Access on August 27, 2026, for 1–4 players. Players build a cabin with room cards, investigate clues, survive monster events, and identify the correct escape room.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **the cabin game**. This trainer reduces the pressure of the cabin game’s card-driven survival loop. It can pause the clock, control monster movement, protect players during event minigames, preserve light sources, and assist with clue investigation while retaining the game’s room-building and deduction mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, practice investigation routes, learn event minigames, test cabin layouts, or reduce the pressure of monsters and the advancing clock.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Clock** — Stops the table clock and prevents time pressure from advancing while players plan card plays, inspect clues, or navigate the cabin.
- **Event Protection** — Prevents failure penalties during first-person event minigames, chases, searches, and other card-triggered encounters.
- **Safe Escape Attempts** — Allows players to test the suspected escape room without ending the session after an incorrect deduction.

### 📌 Survival

- **Monster Freeze** — Stops monster movement and prevents threat markers from closing in during exploration and investigation.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Infinite Light** — Keeps candles and other event lighting available during dark searches and ritual encounters.

### 📌 Investigation

- **Clue Assist** — Helps preserve investigation progress and prevents clue-related progress from being lost during a run.

### 📌 Card System

- **Unlimited Card Actions** — Removes action pressure when placing rooms, using investigation cards, or managing event cards at the round table.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement alternates between sitting at the round table and standing to explore the rooms created by room cards. Use movement-related trainer options before entering a dangerous hallway or starting an event so the clock and monsters do not punish route testing.

### Tools & Inventory

the cabin game does not use a conventional equipment inventory. Players manage cards at the round table and interact with objects found in rooms. Investigation progress is best recorded by room, object, card trigger, and result. Infinite Light is useful for events that require a candle or another limited light source.

### World & Environment

Room cards determine the cabin layout, so connected rooms and escape routes are important. Investigation cards reveal evidence tied to rooms and objects, while Event cards interrupt table play with minigames, chases, darkness, or monster encounters. Use Freeze Clock while studying layouts and Clue Assist when comparing evidence before selecting the escape room.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional weapon-based combat system. Survival depends on movement, hiding, following event rules, and avoiding monsters. Event Protection and Monster Freeze are intended for practicing these encounters without losing the run.
